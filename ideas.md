# Campus Lost & Found — Design Direction

## Direction source
The user explicitly specified a **Purple + Blue + White** professional campus-product theme. This is the chosen direction; no alternative theme is introduced.

## Design dimensions
- **Design movement:** Contemporary editorial SaaS, softened for a welcoming campus community. Clear, calm, and trustworthy rather than playful or institutional bureaucracy.
- **Core principles:** Reunite, don't overwhelm; make reporting and finding immediately obvious; protect personal contact details; show verification and recovery status transparently; keep admin power visible but separate.
- **Color philosophy:** Deep ink-indigo `#242246` anchors trust; vivid violet `#6558E8` identifies primary actions; cobalt `#3478F6` marks discovery and verified states; clean white `#FFFFFF` and cool surface `#F6F7FB` provide space; restrained amber signals review and green signals successful recovery. Maintain AA-friendly text contrast.
- **Layout paradigm:** Airy editorial landing page with a compact top navigation and a calm hero; search-first item discovery; two-column item detail; left-rail dashboards on desktop that collapse into a mobile menu; consistent content width, 8px spacing rhythm, 12-column desktop grid, and stacked mobile cards.
- **Signature elements:** A pair of interlinked finder tags in the Campus Found wordmark; small status pills; category glyphs; a tactile “report an item” card; an explainable match score with factor chips; modular dashboard statistic cards.
- **Interaction philosophy:** Clear labels, visible focus, reversible edits, immediate feedback via toast/inline validation, confirmation for destructive actions, and explicit empty/loading/error states. No fake actions.
- **Animation:** Subtle short fade/translate for page entry and hover lift for actionable cards; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; never delay critical actions.
- **Typography system:** Inter for interface and display hierarchy, system sans-serif fallback; compact bold section headings; readable body copy and monospace report/claim IDs.
- **Brand essence:** A reliable campus service that helps a lost object and its owner find one another.
- **Brand voice:** Helpful, direct, calm, privacy-conscious. Use short action-oriented labels and reassuring status language.
- **Wordmark/logo:** “Campus Found” paired with a simple two-link finder mark. The square app icon uses a full-bleed deep-indigo ground and two interlinked filled-outline loops in white and cobalt; the same mark is used in navigation and favicon.
- **Signature brand color:** Violet `#6558E8`.

## Planned imagery
The interface uses locally bundled factual sample item photos from image search for demonstration cards, not as brand identity. The logo is original geometric artwork. Item-photo use is documented as sample/demo imagery; user uploads supersede it on reports.
